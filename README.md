Real conch physics — a conch is a closed conical-cylindrical resonator, which means it naturally amplifies odd harmonics (1×, 3×, 5×, 7×, 9×, 11×). Each court shapes this differently:

Seelie (moonlight silver) — pure odd partials, bright upper shimmer, bell-like clarity
Unseelie (deep violet) — adds a sub-octave below the fundamental, hollow and dark, emphasizes low mids
Wild (forest green) — more even harmonics, reedy and alive, mid-forward formant

The Breath Bar — drag it before casting to set your breath strength. Louder blow = brighter attack, stronger harmonics. At whisper level it's delicate and airy; at full blow it fills the room.
Eight Spells — different blowing techniques:

Long Blow Q — hold pad, sustained fundamental, slow swell — the classic conch call
Short Blow W — single sharp honk
Trill E — rapid alternation between fundamental and third harmonic, 8 bursts
Overtone R — hold pad, overblown to the 3rd partial plus shimmer LFOs on harmonics 5 through 13
Summon A — rising pitch glide from below the fundamental up to 2.5× with breath noise
Ward S — hold pad, low drone at half-frequency with pulsing harmonic bursts every 0.6s
Lament D — slow descending pitch with vibrato, mournful
Wild Call F — hold pad, multiphonic — two simultaneous pitches a near-fifth apart, the way a skilled player can split the airstream
